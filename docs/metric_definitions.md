# HVAC Performance Hub metric definitions

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Sum of billed revenue for completed jobs. |
| Gross profit | Billed revenue less direct technician labor and material costs. |
| Gross margin | Gross profit divided by billed revenue. |
| Average ticket | Billed revenue divided by completed jobs. |
| Revenue per technician hour | Billed revenue divided by actual labor hours. |
| Labor variance | Actual labor hours less estimated labor hours, divided by estimated labor hours. |
| First-time fix rate | Share of completed jobs resolved on the initial visit. |
| Callback rate | Share of completed jobs that generate a return visit for the same issue. |
| Agreement conversion | Share of eligible completed jobs where a new maintenance agreement was sold. |
| Estimate win rate | Won estimates divided by all estimates sent. |
| Agreement renewal rate | Renewed maintenance agreements divided by agreements eligible for renewal in the synthetic dataset. |

All metrics are calculated from synthetic portfolio data and are not benchmarks for any real HVAC company.
